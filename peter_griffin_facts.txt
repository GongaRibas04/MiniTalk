Here are some fun facts about Peter Griffin from Family Guy:

1.  His full name is Peter Löwenbräu Griffin Sr.
2.  He is of Irish, German, and Mexican descent.
3.  He is the biological son of Thelma Griffin and Mickey McFinnigan, an Irish drunkard.
4.  He was born in Quahog, Rhode Island.
5.  He is the main protagonist of the show Family Guy.
6.  He is known for his signature laugh.
7.  He has held various jobs, including working at the Pawtucket Patriot Brewery and as a fisherman.
